ScholarTasks · scholartasks.com

Kids today can copy and paste an answer in seconds, and they learn nothing. The Homework Helper is the safe alternative. It helps with any subject, from algebra to essays, but it never gives the answer and never writes for the student. Students type the problem, snap a photo of their work, or upload the assignment. It asks what they tried, gives one hint at a time, finds the first mistake in their work, and asks them to explain their thinking. A second AI checks every reply before the student sees it. Parents see what was practiced, how many hints it took, and every time the helper said no. The goal is a student who can think it through on their own, even with the phone switched off.
