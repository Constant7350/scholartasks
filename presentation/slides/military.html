ScholarTasks · scholartasks.com

Some students want to serve their country, and the military can pay for college too. ScholarTasks compares the four ways in: a service academy like West Point or the Naval Academy, an ROTC scholarship at a regular college, a military college like the University of North Georgia, or enlisting with paid job training. It explains how to get a nomination from your senators and representative, puts every step from junior year on the to-do list, and shows the GI Bill and other ways service helps fund school. Families can also pick military academies and colleges in the college search.
