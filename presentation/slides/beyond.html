ScholarTasks · scholartasks.com

Colleges and scholarships also look at activities, leadership, service and recommendation letters. Each school year, ScholarTasks adds the right milestones for that grade to the to-do list. Students log service hours and activities as they go, keep track of who will write their letters, and build a brag sheet for teachers. A quick check-up shows what is already strong and what to work on next, so senior year is not a scramble.
