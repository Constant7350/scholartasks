Students can search almost two thousand colleges, take a quick match quiz, and add a school with one tap. Each college is marked reach, match or likely, based on grades, harder classes and test scores, and shows an estimated cost after state aid and saved scholarships. The essay coach gives feedback, but never writes for the student. And the Common App ready page checks every activity, honor and essay against Common App's limits, so applying is just copy, paste and submit.
