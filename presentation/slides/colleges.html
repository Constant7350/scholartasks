ScholarTasks · scholartasks.com

Students can search more than five thousand colleges, choose public, private or Christian colleges, even by church family, take a quick match quiz, and add a school with one tap. Each college is marked reach, match or likely, based on grades, harder classes and test scores, and shows an estimated cost after state aid and saved scholarships. The essay coach gives feedback on a draft typed in, uploaded from Word, or photographed from paper, and the draft downloads back as a Word file. It never writes for the student. And the Common App ready page checks every activity, honor and essay against Common App's limits, so applying is just copy, paste and submit. Then, for each college, students track their transcript, counselor letter and teacher letters, and can ask their counselor for them right in the app.
