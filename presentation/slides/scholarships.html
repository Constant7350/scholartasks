ScholarTasks · scholartasks.com

Instead of searching a dozen websites, students answer a few questions and see awards sorted into You qualify, Maybe, and Not a fit. Our team checks the list, and the app re-checks award details twice a week during scholarship season. When a student picks one, it goes straight onto their deadline list with a checklist. Essays that ask the same kind of question are bundled, so one strong draft starts the others, and a copy-ready profile has every answer a form asks for.
