ScholarTasks · scholartasks.com

Instead of searching a dozen websites, students answer a few questions. Then they see awards sorted into You qualify, Maybe, and Not a fit. There are hundreds of Georgia and national awards, and the list grows every week. Each new award is checked on its official page before families see it, and re-checked twice a week in scholarship season. Then students get each application ready step by step, right in the app. Each award gets its own page with the questions on the form, the papers and recommendation letters it needs, and a bar that shows what to do next. Short answers they already wrote show up as a starting point, and a copy-ready profile fills in the rest. For your school's own local scholarships, students send the application to the counselor right inside the app. Other awards are sent on the award's own website, and the app keeps track of what was sent and what was won.
