ScholarTasks · scholartasks.com

From time to time, ScholarTasks runs an essay challenge for each grade group, with a fresh topic in a different subject, like science, history or careers. Students write right in the app, step by step, like a game. They jot ideas, make a plan, write a draft, come back another day to improve it, and polish it, earning a badge for each step. Pasting is turned off, and the coach gives tips but never writes, so every word is their own. A parent signs permission in the app. Our review team judges with names hidden, so free and paid accounts have the same chance. Every student who finishes gets a certificate, and winners win cash prizes.
