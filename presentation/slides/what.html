What it is

One app to plan, fund and prepare

A family app with an AI counselor that knows your state’s rules and your student’s own grades, colleges and deadlines.

Plan

College list, deadlines, to-do list, essays, activities and a resume, with reminders that come to you.

Fund

Matched scholarships, state scholarship tracking, FAFSA and state aid checklists, and cost estimates.

Prepare

Grades 6 to 12: GPA scorecard, study skills, Homework Helper, practice questions and SAT and ACT prep.

ScholarTasks · scholartasks.com

Think of it in three parts. Plan: what to do, and when. Fund: how to find and keep the money, from your state's programs to private scholarships. Prepare: grades, study habits and test scores, starting as early as sixth grade.
