ScholarTasks · scholartasks.com

Good grades come from good habits, and those start in middle school. ScholarTasks teaches students how to study, then has them practice each method in math, science, history and English, because understanding an equation takes different practice than remembering dates. They test themselves in every class they take, from grade six math to calculus and eight world language courses, with questions that get harder as they improve, from Core up to Expert. Badges and an optional national ranking, which never shows names, keep even the strongest students pushing. And the tutor explains instead of handing over answers. Families set goals together, Grade Rescue catches a slipping grade early, and course advice helps students take harder classes at the right pace.
