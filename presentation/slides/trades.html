Not every student wants a four-year degree, and many great careers start with a trade. ScholarTasks covers sixty-seven skilled trades, with what each one earns, how long training takes, and what license is needed. That includes well-paid careers many families have never heard of, like CNC machining, power plant operator, ultrasound technician and air traffic controller. Students can find training near home or anywhere in the country, follow step by step apply checklists, and see ways to fund it, like Georgia’s HOPE Grant. And companies anywhere in the country can post apprenticeships, paid training and trade internships for free. Students see openings near home first. Our team checks every listing, nothing about the student is shared, and students choose who to contact, so it opens doors for small businesses and for students who want to start working and learning right away.
