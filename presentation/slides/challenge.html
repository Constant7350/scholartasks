The problem

College planning has too many moving parts

Deadlines everywhere

Colleges, scholarships, FAFSA and state forms all have different dates on different websites.

State aid rules are tricky

State scholarships often count the GPA differently from the report card. Families often find out too late.

Scholarships are scattered

Good awards are spread across hundreds of sites, and many lists are out of date or full of ads.

Counselors are stretched

School counselors care, but each one has many students. Parents end up doing the research at night.

ScholarTasks · scholartasks.com

Most of us have felt this. Deadlines are spread across a dozen websites. State scholarships often count the grade point average differently from the report card. Scholarships are all over the internet. And our school counselors have a lot of students. ScholarTasks pulls all of this into one place.
