For school leaders and counselors: ScholarTasks supports your counselors, it does not replace them. The school portal is free for every school. Counselors see which students filed the FAFSA, have a college list or have overdue deadlines, can upload a class roster under a signed student data agreement to see who has joined, email a whole grade at once, and post local scholarships just for their families. Families choose what to share by entering a school code, and can stop any time. Essays, AI chats and income are never shared. Optional AI packages for whole schools will come later.
