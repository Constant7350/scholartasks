For school leaders and counselors: ScholarTasks supports your counselors. It doesn't replace them. Our school portal is free for every school, and it's its own simple workspace. The Today page shows what needs the counselor right now, each with a one-click button: transcripts and letters to send, seniors who haven't filed the FAFSA, and students who are behind. Students ask for transcripts and counselor letters in the app. Counselors send them the usual way, tap mark sent, and the family sees it. Counselors can email a whole grade, post local scholarships that students apply for right in the app, and add school events like FAFSA night that families can put on their calendar. Families choose what to share with a school code, and can stop any time. Essays, AI chats and income are never shared, unless a student chooses to send an essay with an application for the school's own scholarship.
