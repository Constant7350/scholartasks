ScholarTasks · scholartasks.com

Here's how a family uses it over the years, in three stages. Explore, in grades six to eight: the career quiz, a dream board, grades, study skills and a Homework Helper that explains instead of answering. Build, in grades nine and ten: course choices, activities, the PSAT, early scholarships and a first look at colleges. Apply, in grades eleven and twelve: test prep, the college list, essays, scholarships and aid forms. The app shows each student only their stage's pages, so a seventh grader never sees a FAFSA checklist. Parents can switch on every page any time, and the full app opens by itself in grade eleven.
