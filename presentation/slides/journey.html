How families use it

One app from grade 8 to graduation

Grades 6–9

  • Start the GPA scorecard
  • Learn study skills
  • Set family goals
  • Pick the right courses

Grade 10

  • Track HOPE GPA
  • Add rigor courses
  • Explore majors and trades
  • Start test practice

Grade 11

  • SAT and ACT prep
  • Build a college list
  • See real costs
  • Line up recommenders

Grade 12

  • Applications and essays
  • Scholarships
  • FAFSA and GSFAPP
  • Deadline reminders

A typical week: the student updates a grade and ticks off a task; the parent checks the home screen in two minutes.

ScholarTasks · scholartasks.com

Here is how a family uses it over the years. In sixth and seventh grade, a parent and student start with study habits and the career quiz. In eighth and ninth grade it is mostly the GPA scorecard, study skills and choosing courses. By tenth grade students are tracking HOPE, adding harder classes and logging activities. Eleventh grade is test prep and the college list. Senior year is applications, scholarships and aid forms, with reminders keeping everyone on time. Week to week, it only takes a few minutes.
