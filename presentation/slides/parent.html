The family account

Parents stay in control, students do the work

Parent app

  • Owns and manages the account
  • Sees every student in one place
  • Chooses what each student can change
  • Gets the same reminders as the student
  • Can add a second parent

Student app

  • Joins with a code from the parent
  • Their own login on their own phone
  • Keeps grades, lists and to-dos current
  • Asks the AI counselor questions
  • At 18, chooses what parents can still see

ScholarTasks · scholartasks.com

This is one of the biggest differences from other apps. The parent owns the account and decides what each student can change. The student has their own app and does the day-to-day work. When a student turns 18, they get a say in what parents can still see, which respects their privacy as adults.
