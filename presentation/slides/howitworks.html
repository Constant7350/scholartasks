How it works

Set up in four easy steps

1

A parent signs up

With Google, email or a password. The parent owns the account.

2

Add each student

Name, grade and state. Try the tools first; add students when ready.

3

Send a join code

One tap to text or email it. The student joins on their own phone.

4

Home shows what's next

Approve, check off a to-do or ask right from Home. Long pages open one part at a time.

ScholarTasks · scholartasks.com

Getting started takes a few minutes. A parent signs up and adds a student. The app makes a short join code. You tap Text or Email to send it, and your student types it in on their own phone. From then on, everything the family adds lands in one shared plan. And every day, the Home page shows what's next. Parents approve things with one tap, anyone can check off a to-do or ask the AI counselor right there, and a search in the menu finds any page. Longer pages have icons at the top, so you open one part at a time instead of scrolling.
