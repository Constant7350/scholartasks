ScholarTasks · scholartasks.com

Families live on their phones, so ScholarTasks does too. The iPhone and Android apps are in testing now, and the website works on any device. Sign-in is quick with Face ID or a fingerprint, which helps, because teenagers forget passwords. Remember me keeps you signed in at home, and on a shared computer you just uncheck it. What you add on the phone shows up on the computer too. The top bar stays put on every page, so Home, help and the menu are always one tap away. The menu shows every page as colored tiles in the order of the journey, and a search box finds any page. Tap the question mark and small help balloons appear, with a search box that answers from the manual.
