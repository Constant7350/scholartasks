ScholarTasks · scholartasks.com

Families live on their phones, so ScholarTasks does too. The iPhone and Android apps are in testing now, and the website works on any device. Sign-in is quick with Face ID or a fingerprint, which matters because teenagers forget passwords. Remember me keeps you signed in at home, and unticking it signs you out on a shared computer. Whatever you add on the phone shows up on the computer, and the other way around. The menu stays short: only the headings show until you tap one, and every page has a link back to its section, so nobody has to scroll to find their way.
