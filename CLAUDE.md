# ScholarTasks

## Keep the presentation current (standing rule)

Any PR that adds or changes a feature families or schools would notice must also update the parent and school
presentation in the same PR: the slide, its narration, then the build. Steps are in `presentation/README.md`.
Do this without being asked.

## Toolbox

Reusable building blocks for all our apps live in the repo
github.com/Constant7350/app-toolbox. Look there first before building
something it may already hold. When a piece of work here produces something
another app could use, add or update its block there in the same piece of
work (README with What it is, Why this way, Rules, Where it lives, plus a
copy in code/; never keys or customer data).
